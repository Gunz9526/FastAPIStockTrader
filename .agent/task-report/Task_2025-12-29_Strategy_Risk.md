# Task Report: Strategy & Risk Management Implementation

**Date**: 2025-12-29
**Task**: Integrate TA-Lib and Risk Management

## Summary
Enhanced the trading system's intelligence and safety. Integrated `TA-Lib` (v0.6.8) for robust feature engineering and implemented a `RiskManager` to enforce capital controls and exit strategies.

## Changes Created
### Feature Engineering (`app/ml/features.py`)
- **TA-Lib Integration**: Added `add_technical_indicators` method.
- **Indicators**: RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, SMA, EMA, ATR.
- **Data Handling**: Auto-cleaning of NaNs generated by lag indicators.

### Risk Management (`app/services/risk_manager.py`)
- **Position Sizing**: Calculates max allowable quantity based on `max_position_size_pct` (default 10%).
- **Safety Checks**: Blocks orders if buying power is insufficient or risk limits are hit.
- **Exit Strategy**: Calculates Stop-Loss (2%) and Take-Profit (5%) levels.

### Strategy Engine (`app/services/trading_strategy.py`)
- **Pipeline**: Data -> Features(TA-Lib) -> ML Prediction -> Risk Validation -> Execution.
- **Refactoring**: Now utilizes the new helper classes for modular logic.

### Tests
- `tests/test_strategy.py`: Unit tests for Risk logic and Feature generation.

## Status
- **Strategy**: Advanced (Rule-based Risk + ML Prediction + TA-Lib Features).
- **Dependencies**: Updated with `ta-lib` binary wheels support.
